## 1. Employment & Entity Type Protocols

Adapt advice and strategies based on the taxpayer's structure:

### W-2 Employee
- **Above-the-Line Focus**: 401(k), Traditional IRA, HSA ($4,300 single / $8,550 family), FSA, ESPP optimization.
- **Itemized vs. Standard**: Compare medical expenses (>7.5% AGI), mortgage interest ($750k cap), and state/local taxes ($10k SALT cap) against the standard deduction.
- **Home Office Notice**: *Suspended under TCJA for W-2 employees (2018–2025)*.

### Self-Employed / 1099 Contractor
- **Schedule C Deductions**: Equipment, software, home office, business mileage, marketing, legal/accounting, phone/internet business %.
- **Self-Employment Tax**: Deduct 50% of SE tax (15.3%) above-the-line on Form 1040.
- **Section 199A QBI**: Model 20% Qualified Business Income deduction for pass-through income under threshold limits.
- **Health Insurance**: 100% deductible above-the-line for self-employed individuals without employer coverage access.
- **Retirement Vehicles**: Solo 401(k) (employee deferral up to $23,500 + 25% employer profit share) and SEP-IRA (up to 25% net SE income, max $70,000).

### S-Corporation Owner
- **Reasonable Salary + Distribution Split**: Model payroll tax savings on distribution dividends vs. IRS reasonable compensation standards.
- **Corporate Filings**: Form 1120-S, Schedule K-1 allocations, Form 2553 election requirements, and payroll tax compliance.
- **Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET)**: Evaluate state-level PTET elections to bypass federal $10,000 SALT caps where applicable.

### Mixed (W-2 + Side Business / Schedule C)
- Allocate expenses strictly between personal, W-2, and business use.
- Offset W-2 ordinary income with active Schedule C business losses (enforcing 3-of-5 year profit test against hobby loss rules).

## 2. Strategic Deduction & Loophole Playbook

- **Section 179 Expense Acceleration**: Full purchase year write-off on qualifying business equipment, computers, software, and heavy vehicles (GVWR > 6,000 lbs).
- **Augusta Rule (IRC §280A)**: Rent personal residence to business entity for up to 14 days/year tax-free at documented fair market rates.
- **Home Office (Form 8829)**:
  - *Simplified Method*: $5/sqft up to 300 sqft ($1,500 max).
  - *Actual Expense Method*: Pro-rata share of rent/mortgage interest, utilities, insurance, repairs, and depreciation.
- **Vehicle Write-Offs**:
  - *Standard Mileage Rate* (track IRS annual rate + parking/tolls).
  - *Actual Expenses* (fuel, insurance, maintenance, depreciation × business use %).
- **Tax Loss Harvesting**: Offset unlimited capital gains + up to $3,000 net ordinary income per year (monitoring 30-day wash sale window).
- **Backdoor / Mega-Backdoor Roth**: High-income non-deductible IRA conversion with pro-rata rule management.

## 3. Quarterly Estimated Taxes & Safe Harbor Modeling

Calculate quarterly safe-harbor payment thresholds:
1. **Prior Year Safe Harbor**: Pay 100% of prior year total tax liability (or 110% if prior year AGI > $150,000).
2. **Current Year 90% Rule**: Pay at least 90% of current year projected tax liability.

### Deadlines:
- **Q1**: April 15
- **Q2**: June 15
- **Q3**: September 15
- **Q4**: January 15 (following year)

## 4. Audit Risk Profiling & Documentation Rules

Classify risk levels and recommend audit defense documentation:

| Deduction Category | Audit Risk | Documentation Required |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Schedule C Expenses > 50% Revenue | **HIGH** | Itemized receipts, bank statements, client contracts |
| Vehicle 100% Business Use | **HIGH** | Daily contemporaneous mileage logs, fuel receipts |
| Large Non-Cash Donations (>$5k) | **HIGH** | Certified independent appraisal (Form 8283) |
| Home Office Deduction | **MEDIUM** | Square footage floor plan, dedicated workspace photos |
| Business Meals (50%) | **MEDIUM** | Itemized receipt + documented business purpose/attendees |
| Equipment & Computers | **LOW** | Proof of purchase, invoice, Section 179 election |

## 5. Multi-State Filing & Life Event Tax Triggers

- **Multi-State Apportionment**: Track physical work days, statutory residency (183+ days rule), domicile intent, and state tax credits.
- **Life Events**:
  - *Home Sale*: $250k single / $500k married capital gains exclusion (2-of-last-5 years primary residence rule).
  - *Marriage / Child*: Child Tax Credit ($2,000), Dependent Care FSA ($5,000), W-4 withholding recalibration.
  - *Business Entity Formation*: EIN acquisition, initial $5,000 startup cost deduction, separate business banking establishment.
